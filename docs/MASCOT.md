# Mascot: design brief and picture prompt

FDE has no mascot yet. This is the brief for making one (with an image generator or an artist).
Check the licence of whatever you use before adding it to the repository; the project is MIT.

## The character

An "OS-tan": a girl who personifies the desktop, in the manga / anime style of the early-2000s
OS-tans. Working name **Aero-chan** (free to change).

- Looks: a friendly girl of about 16, long straight sky-blue hair with a white-to-aqua gradient
  at the ends, big bright blue-green eyes, a gentle open smile, a light blush.
- Hair: two glossy glass orbs as hair clips (one green, one blue), and a small green sprout
  (a leaf pair) on top of her head.
- Outfit: a sailor-style top in white and sky blue with a translucent glass-blue collar, a green
  ribbon bow with a glossy round orb brooch (like the start orb), a short blue pleated skirt with
  white stripes, white thigh-high socks, white and green shoes.
- Props (optional): a floating, see-through glass window panel in each hand, like the glass
  windows of the desktop.
- Mood: cheerful, clean, optimistic, a bit nostalgic.

## Style

Frutiger Aero: glossy, glassy highlights, soft gradients, lens flare, bubbles, water droplets,
fresh greens and sky blues. Clean line art, cel shading with soft gradient shadows, high detail
in the eyes and hair.

Background: a bright blue sky with white clouds over a green rolling hill, in the style of a
classic desktop wallpaper (but not any real wallpaper).

Do not copy any existing character (Microsoft's, or any artist's OS-tan). Original design only.

## Picture prompt (for Gemini or another image generator)

> Anime / manga illustration of an original OS-tan character, a cheerful girl of about 16 with
> long straight sky-blue hair fading to aqua at the tips, big glossy blue-green eyes, a gentle
> open smile and a light blush. Two glossy glass orb hair clips (one green, one blue) and a small
> green sprout of two leaves on top of her head. She wears a white and sky-blue sailor-style top
> with a translucent glass-blue collar, a green ribbon bow with a glossy round orb brooch, a
> short blue pleated skirt with white stripes, white thigh-high socks, white and green shoes. She
> holds two floating transparent glass window panels in her hands, glowing softly. Frutiger Aero
> aesthetic: glossy highlights, soft gradients, lens flare, floating bubbles and water droplets.
> Background: bright blue sky, white clouds, a green rolling hill. Clean line art, cel shading
> with soft gradient shadows, detailed eyes and hair, full body, dynamic pose, high quality
> illustration, 3:4 portrait. No text, no logos, no real brand names.

Variants worth asking for: a head-and-shoulders crop for the repository card (1280x640, with her
on the right and room for the title on the left), a small square version for an avatar, and a
chibi version for stickers.

## Where it goes

Save the picture as `assets/mascot.png` (or `.webp`), then add it to the top of `README.md` and
use it for the repository card (Settings > Social preview). Tell me and I will wire it in.
